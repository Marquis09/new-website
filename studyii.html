Working Class Heroes Website Redesign - UX Case Study

Improving the User Experience and Mobile Usability for a "SEAMLESS SHOPPING JOURNEY".

📁 1. Process Overview

Process Index: Process Overview | Project Summary | Pain Points | Design Process | Key Solutions | Before & After | Reflection | Key Learnings

Executive Summary

  • Overview: This project challenged me to rethink how users interact with an e‑commerce website and how subtle usability issues can create major friction. I approached the redesign with a strong focus on clarity, predictability, and user comfort.
  • Challenge: The original site suffered from cluttered navigation, inconsistent layouts, and poor mobile responsiveness. My goal was to modernise the aesthetic while creating a smoother, more intuitive shopping experience.
  • Outcome: The redesign resulted in clearer navigation, improved mobile usability, and a streamlined checkout flow that reduces hesitation and supports confident purchasing.

Project Details

  • My Role: UX/UI Designer
  • Duration: 4 Weeks
  • Tools: Figma, Adobe Photoshop, HTML, CSS (Bootstrap)
  • Project Type: "E-COMMERCE REDESIGN"

📁 2. Project Summary & The Challenge

The original Working Class Heroes e‑commerce website suffered from significant usability issues that directly impacted user confidence and conversion rates. Navigation felt cluttered, product information was difficult to scan, and the checkout flow created unnecessary friction for users trying to complete purchases.

⚠ The Challenge: My goal was to redesign the experience in a way that felt modern, predictable, and supportive of fast decision‑making. I wanted users to feel guided rather than overwhelmed, especially on mobile devices where clarity and responsiveness are essential.

This project also reflects my personal design philosophy: creating interfaces that feel intuitive, calm, and visually structured. Even within a commercial context, I aim to reduce cognitive load and help users move through tasks with confidence.

📁 3. Problem Statement & Design Goals

User analysis revealed that the website's technical and structural shortcomings directly impacted customer abandonment rates.

The Core Pain Points

  • Cluttered UI: Lack of clear navigation and "CONFUSING HOMEPAGE DESIGN".
  • Mobile Frustration: Poor mobile responsiveness for "ON-THE-GO USERS".
  • Checkout Friction: Inconsistent layout, particularly with product availability and "STREAMLINED CHECKOUT PROCESS".
  • Missing Essentials: Missing footer with important links like contact and social media.

Success Metrics (Goals)

  • Navigation: Improve homepage by adding clear sections and a welcoming introduction.
  • Responsiveness: Make the website "MOBILE-RESPONSIVE" for on-the-go users.
  • Usability: Incorporate a functional search bar with error prevention and "SUGGESTION PROMPTS".
  • Conversion: Revamp product pages by adding "REAL-TIME STOCK AVAILABILITY".

📁 4. Design Process: Analysis and Ideation

The redesign followed a user-centered approach, prioritizing "INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE" and mobile adaptability.

Heuristic Review

Conducted a heuristic evaluation to identify violations of usability principles, highlighting the severe navigation and "ERROR-PREVENTION" issues.

Wireframing Focus

Designed low-fidelity wireframes, focusing on decluttering the homepage and ensuring the core shopping flow worked flawlessly on "MOBILE VIEWPORTS".

Visual Design & Prototyping

Developed high-fidelity prototypes incorporating modern e-commerce visual standards, including clearer product imagery and "STREAMLINED TYPOGRAPHY".

Before finalising the redesign, I explored visual directions that balanced modern e-commerce standards with a clean, approachable aesthetic. I focused on neutral tones, simplified typography, and clear product imagery to create a visual language that felt trustworthy and easy to navigate.

📁 5. Key Design Solutions Implemented

Each design decision in this project was made to reduce friction and support a smoother shopping journey. I prioritised clarity, predictability, and responsiveness because users visiting e-commerce sites often make quick decisions. By simplifying the homepage, strengthening the search experience, and refining the checkout flow, I aimed to create an environment where users could browse confidently and complete purchases without hesitation.

📁 7. Final Thoughts & Future Steps

This redesign was a valuable experience, as it allowed me to tackle real-world usability issues and improve the overall shopping experience.

Future Steps

  • A/B Testing: Conduct usability tests on the redesigned checkout flow to "VALIDATE THE REDUCTION IN FRICTION".
  • Dynamic Features: Integrate personalized recommendations and "INTEGRATED CUSTOMER REVIEWS".
  • Refinement: Continue refining the site with more dynamic features, like personalized recommendations.

Reflection

This project strengthened my confidence in handling complex UX flows and balancing user needs with business goals. Working on an e‑commerce redesign pushed me to think more critically about how small usability issues can create large‑scale friction. I learned to trust my judgement, make decisive design choices, and refine interactions that directly influence user behaviour.

What I Learned

  • Clear navigation dramatically reduces hesitation during browsing.
  • Mobile-first thinking is essential for fast, on the go shopping behaviour.
  • Predictive search and error prevention build user trust.
  • Checkout friction is often caused by small layout inconsistencies.
  • Visual calmness improves user confidence during decision-making.

How This Project Helped Me Grow

This redesign helped me refine my ability to simplify complex flows, communicate design decisions clearly, and create experiences that feel predictable and user-friendly. It also strengthened my confidence in applying UX principles to real-world commercial challenges.
